Our Legacy

A Heritage Rooted
in the Sacred City

Since 1966, VMGN Fashions Private Limited has been the cornerstone of wholesale ethnic fashion in India, rooted in the ancient city of Varanasi — the spiritual capital renowned for its exquisite Banarasi textiles.

Today, proudly in its third generation, The GN Group carries this heritage forward with renewed vigour — blending the soul of Banarasi craftsmanship with modern manufacturing excellence. From the historic lanes of Golghar, we have grown into a trusted wholesale powerhouse for the nation's largest retail chains.

✓  Currently open for wholesale enquiries. Whether you are a large-format retailer, a regional chain, or a fashion brand seeking authentic ethnic fashion at scale — we are ready to work with you.

Our e-commerce venture, golghar.org, brings this same Banarasi heritage directly to discerning customers worldwide.

Our Capabilities

Built for Scale

VMGN Fashions operates at a scale designed to serve India's largest retail enterprises — infrastructure, partnerships, and manufacturing capabilities that position us as the preferred wholesale partner.

01

State-of-the-Art Manufacturing

Our Value Addition Center (VAC) at Rajatab, Varanasi is the first garment manufacturing unit of its kind in Eastern Uttar Pradesh, built in collaboration with the Government of India.

02

Empowering Thousands

We employ thousands of skilled artisans, stitchers, and job workers from rural villages, preserving traditional craftsmanship while providing sustainable livelihoods.

03

Indian Handloom Brand

Registered with the prestigious Indian Handloom Brand, certifying our commitment to authentic, handcrafted textiles representing India's rich weaving heritage.

04

LIVA Partnership

Proud partners with Aditya Birla Group for LIVA fabric sarees, combining premium materials with our craftsmanship expertise for superior product offerings.

05

Government Recognition

Our manufacturing excellence earned recognition from Prime Minister Narendra Modi, who met our Director Shri Mukund Ballabh Agrawal in February 2020.

06

Bulk Order Specialists

Equipped to handle large-volume orders for national retail chains. Streamlined production and logistics ensure timely delivery at scale without compromising quality.
